## Design (locked per brand brief)
- Paper editorial aesthetic. Surfaces: paper `#F4F1EC`, paper-warm `#EDE8DF`, ink `#1E1E1C`. Accents (one per section): redbrown `#7A4A3A`, indigo `#4A5C6A`, gold `#9A8F6A`.
- Typography: Literata (serif) for hero/headlines/body/quotes, Inter (sans) for eyebrows, meta, buttons, navigation.
- Hairline rules as primary structural device; subtle paper noise on body.
- Forbidden: drop shadows, non-noise gradients, border-radius > 6px, tropical color schemes, icon-driven layouts, stock photography.
- Voice law on this surface: zero em dashes, zero exclamation marks, no tourism vocabulary, no conversion-marketing tropes. CTAs read as observations.
- Audience: David — returning serious traveler about to commit. Not "travelers, digital nomads, expats" generically.
